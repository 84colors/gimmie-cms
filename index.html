Our process makes for a smooth experience

Our seamless production process is built around four simple steps, designed to keep everything clear, collaborative, and stress-free from start to finish.

Your feedback is included at every keystage, so there are no surprises - just a smooth process and a final video thatdelivers the results you need.

1.
Script
We transform your message into a clear, concise script that simplifies complex ideas and turns them into an engaging story.
2.
Design
We create custom visuals, characters, colour palettes, and layouts designed specifically for your brand and audience.
3.
Motion
We bring your ideas to life through animation that makes information easy to understand and enjoyable to watch. Live action can also be blended with animation or used on its own.
4.
Voiceover & Music
We add professional voiceover and carefully selected music to strengthen the message and complete the experience.

Freqently asked questions

How much does a video cost?
Every project is different, so pricing depends on the style, length and complexity of the video. After a quick chat about your goals, we’ll provide a clear quote tailored to your project and budget.
How long does it take?
Most projects take around 4 weeks once the deposit has been paid and the script approved, depending on the scope of the project and feedback turnaround times.
Who owns the copyright?
Once the project is completed and fully paid for, the final video is yours to use across your website, social media, presentations and marketing channels.
Do I need to provide my own script?
Only if you’d like to. We can handle the scriptwriting for you and turn your ideas or existing material into a clear, engaging story.
How do we handle revisions?
Your feedback is included throughout the process. Two rounds of revisions are included to help make sure the final video feels just right.
